1. Introduction

Zypher ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting player privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how 2048+ handles data and confirms our strict policy against collecting, transmitting, or sharing personal information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

2048+ operates under a strict zero-data-collection philosophy. We do not collect, harvest, monitor, or transmit:

  • Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or user identifiers.
  • Device Identifiers: No Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), Android ID, IMEI, or hardware serial numbers.
  • Location Data: No GPS, cellular, or network location coordinates.
  • Financial or Payment Details: No payment details or purchase tracking; the game is free to play with no in-app purchases.
  • Usage Telemetry: No analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) tracking your in-game taps, tile merges, or gameplay habits.

3. Device Permissions & Sandbox Isolation

2048+ is engineered to run with complete isolation from sensitive device capabilities:

Zero Dangerous Permissions Guarantee: 2048+ requests zero dangerous permissions in its Android application manifest. Specifically, the app requests no INTERNET permission, no camera access, no contacts access, and no external storage permissions. It operates strictly inside the operating system's internal application sandbox.

4. Local Data Storage & User Control

To preserve your best high scores, current board layout, move undo history, customizable visual themes, and sound effect preferences, 2048+ stores data locally on your device via standard local storage (SharedPreferences).

  • This data is stored purely on-device and is never uploaded, synchronized to external servers, or transmitted to third parties.
  • You retain complete control: clearing app data through your device settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored records.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

2048+ contains no advertisements and integrates no third-party SDKs that monitor user activity, profile behavior, or serve targeted marketing.

6. Network Connectivity

The game is designed to function 100% offline. No internet connection is required or utilized to slide, merge tiles, or enjoy any game features.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because 2048+ does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The application is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with a revised "Effective Date". Continued use of the application signifies acceptance of any updated policy terms.
