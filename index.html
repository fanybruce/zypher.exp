Studio Disclosures

🔒 Studio-Wide Privacy Commitment

Across all applications developed by Zypher, our commitment to player privacy is absolute and grounded in four pillars:

1. Zero Data Brokerage We do not collect, buy, sell, or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.
2. On-Device Processing Features like camera photos and custom imagery remain strictly inside your device sandbox.
3. Offline-First Architecture Our core games require no network connections, logins, or background telemetry.
4. User Data Sovereignty Game stats reside exclusively on your phone. Deleting the app cleanly purges all data.
